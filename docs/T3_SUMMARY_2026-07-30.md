## 3d. Q2, which had never been run

Q2 appears in no earlier day summary and would have been an empty section in the
final report. `scripts/run_q2_crossings.py` answers it on four days.

Two definitional choices, both load-bearing. The flip is the **frozen** map's,
held piecewise constant between recompute points — Q2 asks about the frozen
map's usefulness, so the actual map would answer a different question. And a
crossing is **spot moving across a held level, never the level stepping across
spot**: the frozen flip jumps at 15:45 when the aggregate drops 0DTE per §9.1,
and counting that as a crossing would have inflated every number here.

| bucket | raw n | /day | days to 40 | **episodes** | **days to 40 episodes** | recross <30m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0DTE | 24 | 6.0 | 7 | **4** | **40** | 83% |
| 1-7DTE | 10 | 2.5 | 16 | 1 | 160 | 90% |
| 8-30DTE | **0** | 0 | n/a | 0 | n/a | — |
| aggregate | 12 | 3.0 | 13 | 3 | 53 | 75% |

**The raw count is the wrong denominator for the spec's own question.** With
75–90% recrossing inside 30 minutes, 40 raw crossings is nowhere near 40
independent observations. On episodes — crossings separated by more than the
recross window — 0DTE accrues **1.0 per day and n = 40 needs about 40 trading
days.** Two months, not one week. That is the number the final report should
carry.

**8-30DTE recorded zero crossings in four days.** Its frozen flip sits near 7500
and spot never approached it. In this window that bucket's flip was
descriptively inert — worth stating plainly rather than leaving as a blank row.

Realized range after a crossing against before: 1.09 (0DTE), 0.51 (1-7DTE), 0.67
(aggregate). Reported, not interpreted. The spec forbids significance claims at
this n and the recross rates alone would make any claim unwise.

## 4. The lesson, which outlives the findings

**T2 produced three findings from three or four observations each. T3 broke all
three.** The main line — net GEX mechanical, surface damping, the flip
surface-driven — was built on 24 rows across three different markets and did not
move.

The difference is not luck. It is that the main line was measured across
conditions that varied, while each retracted finding was a streak counted within
one condition, or a shape measured on one day. Both were reported with the same
confidence. They should not have been.

Concretely, for anything drawn from a handful of observations: **state the
number of observations in the same sentence as the claim**, and do not write it
into the handoff's settled decisions until it has survived a day whose data
quality differs from the day it was found on.
